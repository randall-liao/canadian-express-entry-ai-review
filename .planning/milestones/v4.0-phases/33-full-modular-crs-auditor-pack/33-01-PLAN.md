Generalize the shipped Phase-32 single-factor audit slice into the reusable **dispatch spine** that fans one proven verdict contract out across the whole CRS grid, and land the reference **core human capital** module on top of it. Purpose: Satisfy AUDIT-03 (four context-light modules through the frozen primitives, no new trust path) by turning `audit_factor` into a dispatch spine + the first (core) module; satisfy AUDIT-01 (exactly one verdict per factor), AUDIT-04 (factor cards), and AUDIT-05 (substantiated total vs ITA cutoff). This is the interface-first spine — Waves 2-3 fill spouse/transferability/additional deciders into the stub files created here.

Output: `crawler/auditors/` package (dispatch + core module + three conservative stubs), a generalized `crawler/auditor.py`, a full-run report writer, the `vault audit --all` verb, the shared `_contract.md` spec, and dispatch/cutoff tests.
