confirmed sibling below the grounded threshold. Points are read from the grounded row via `_grounded_points`
(never a `15` literal) and the threshold from the grounded row (never an `18` literal) — so no age/points/
date literal is inlined in Python. Missing/unparseable/no-ITA-date/grounding-failure cases fail closed to
`GAP`/`AT-RISK`.
