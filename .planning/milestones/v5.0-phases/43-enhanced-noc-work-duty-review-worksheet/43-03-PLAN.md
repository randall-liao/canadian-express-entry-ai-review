<objective>
Wire the Plan-01 worksheet generator and the Plan-02 inert sidecar into the CLI as
`vault noc-worksheet` and `vault noc-review` subverbs (NOC-01/NOC-02 surface), reusing the
shipped PII-light envelope helpers and the shipped per-field `vault confirm` verb. Human
duty detail routes to stderr; `--json` stdout carries only PII-light dicts. Both verbs
preflight the corpus index and fail closed to one `invalid_input`/exit-2 refusal before any
compute, guaranteeing zero artifacts on refusal. Alias the sidecar's `duty_review_manual`
reason to the shipped `unresolved_noc` remediation action (no duplicate template).

Purpose: NOC-01 requires the applicant can VIEW the cited worksheet; NOC-02 requires per-duty
confirm/correct/reject. This plan is the operator surface — with fixed output containment
(vault-only artifact/detail, PII-light JSON) and no bulk confirm-all (structural rubber-stamp
defense).

Output: the two `vault` subverbs and their command handlers in `crawler/cli.py`, one
`remediation.py` reason alias (`duty_review_manual` -> `unresolved_noc`), and end-to-end CLI
tests in `crawler/tests/test_noc_review_cli.py`.
</objective>
