## Full text
The Minister of Citizenship and Immigration gives the annexed _Ministerial Instructions Respecting Invitations to Apply for Permanent Residence Under the Express Entry System (French-Language proficiency 2026-Version 2)_ under subparagraph 10.3(1)(h.1)(iii)Footnote a, paragraphs 10.3(1)(h.2) to (j)Footnote a and subsection 10.3(1.1)Footnote b of the _Immigration and Refugee Protection Act_ Footnote c.
Ottawa, **May 28, 2026**
Lena Metlege Diab  
Minister of Citizenship and Immigration
### Ministerial Instructions Respecting Invitations to Apply for Permanent Residence Under the Express Entry System (French-Language proficiency 2026-Version 2)
### Definitions
**1** The following definitions apply in these Instructions.
_**eligible foreign national**_ means a foreign national whose expression of interest is in the express entry pool established by the Express Entry Instructions. (_étranger admissible_)
_**Express Entry Instructions**_ means the _Ministerial Instructions Respecting the Express Entry System_ as published in the _Canada Gazette_ , Part I, on December 1, 2014, and as amended from time to time. (_Instructions concernant le système Entrée express_)
_**invitation**_ has the same meaning as in section 1 of the Express Entry Instructions. (_invitation_)
_**Regulations**_ means the _Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations_. (_Règlement_)
### Category
**2** The French-Language proficiency (2026-Version 2) category is established, for the purposes of ranking, for the period set out in section 6.
### Member of category
**3** An eligible foreign national is a member of the category established in section 2 if they are not a member of the class referred to in paragraph 2(d) of the Express Entry Instructions and if they meet the following requirements:
  1. their proficiency in French was assessed using a language test, the results of which are less than two years old, that is approved under subsection 74(3) of the Regulations and provided by an institution or organization designated under that subsection; and
  2. the result for each language skill area tested was a level 7 or higher according to the benchmarks set out in the _Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadiens_ , as defined in section 2 of the Regulations.

### Grouping
**4** For the purpose of ranking, a grouping of eligible foreign nationals is established that consists of those who are eligible to be members of the category established in section 2.
### Economic goal
**5** The economic goal that the Minister seeks to support in establishing the category referred to in section 2 is economic growth through Francophone immigration outside of Quebec.
### Invitations — period
**6** Invitations may be issued under these Instructions during the period beginning on May 28, 2026 and ending on May 29, 2026.
### Invitations — number
**7** The number of invitations that may be issued to eligible foreign nationals in the grouping established in section 4 is 4,500.
### Invitations — required rank
**8** Invitations may be issued to eligible foreign nationals who rank among the first 4,500 eligible foreign nationals in the ranking determined for the eligible foreign nationals in the grouping established in section 4.
