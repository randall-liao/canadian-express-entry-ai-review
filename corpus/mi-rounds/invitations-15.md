The Minister of Citizenship and Immigration gives the annexed _Ministerial Instructions Respecting Invitations to Apply for Permanent Residence Under the Express Entry System (Healthcare and Social Services Occupations, 2026-Version 3)_ under subparagraph 10.3(1)(h.1)(iii)Footnote a, paragraphs 10.3(1)(h.2) to (j)Footnote a and subsection 10.3(1.1)Footnote b of the _Immigration and Refugee Protection Act_ Footnote c.

### Ministerial Instructions Respecting Invitations to Apply for Permanent Residence Under the Express Entry System (Healthcare and Social Services Occupations, 2026-Version 3)
### Definitions
**1** The following definitions apply in these Instructions.
_**eligible foreign national**_ means a foreign national whose expression of interest is in the express entry pool established by the Express Entry Instructions. (_étranger admissible_)
_**Express Entry Instructions**_ means the _Ministerial Instructions Respecting the Express Entry System_ as published in the _Canada Gazette_ , Part I, on December 1, 2014, and as amended from time to time. (_Instructions concernant le système Entrée express_)
_**full-time work**_ has the same meaning as in subsection 73(1) of the Regulations. (_travail à temps plein_)
_**invitation**_ has the same meaning as in section 1 of the Express Entry Instructions. (_invitation_)
_**National Occupational Classification**_ has the same meaning as in section 2 of the Regulations. (_Classification nationale des professions_)
_**Regulations**_ means the _Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations_. (_Règlement_)
_**work**_ has the same meaning as in subsection 73(2) of the Regulations. (_travail_)
### Category
**2** The Healthcare and Social Services Occupations (Version 3) category is established, for the purposes of ranking, for the period set out in section 7.
### Member of category
**3** An eligible foreign national is a member of the category established in section 2 if they are not a member of the class referred to in paragraph 2(d) of the Express Entry Instructions and if they meet the following requirements:
  1. within the previous three years, they have acquired, at least one year of full-time work experience — or the equivalent in part-time work — in one of the occupations referred to in the table to this section;
  2. during that period of work experience they performed the actions described in the lead statement for the occupation as set out in the occupational descriptions of the National Occupational Classification; and
  3. during that period of work experience they performed a substantial number of the main duties of the occupation as set out in the occupational descriptions of the National Occupational Classification, including all of the essential duties.

### Unauthorized work in Canada
**4** A period of unauthorized work in Canada is not to be included in the calculation of the period of work experience referred to in paragraph 3(a).
### Grouping
**5** For the purposes of ranking, a grouping of eligible foreign nationals is established that consists of those who are eligible to be members of the category established in section 2.
### Economic goal
**6** The economic goal that the Minister seeks to support in establishing the category referred to in section 2 is to contribute to Canada’s long-term economic success by addressing long-term labour market shortages.
### Invitations — period
**7** Invitations may be issued under these Instructions during the period beginning on February 20, 2026 and ending on February 21, 2026.
### Invitations — number
**8** The number of invitations that may be issued to eligible foreign nationals in the grouping established in section 5 is 4,000.
### Invitations — required rank
**9** Invitations may be issued to eligible foreign nationals who rank among the first 4,000 eligible foreign nationals in the ranking determined for the eligible foreign nationals in the grouping established in section 5.
