# How to get a police certificate – Angola
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your biometrics (fingerprints and photo) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Certificado do registo criminal (Certificate of Criminal Record)
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for
  * permanent residence

See the document checklist or visa office requirements for details.
## How to apply
Apply in person or by mail at the Ministry of Justice.
You can authorize a representative (Angolan citizens) to apply on your behalf.
## Documents you will need
  * Application forms which can be obtained from the respective office
  * Valid identity document (identity card or driving license)
  * Valid passport
  * Citizen proof/nationality card

### For foreign nationals
  * A copy of your permit of stay in Angola 
    * where it was issued
  * Passport number
  * Proofs of legal stay 
    * A copy of passport held during the said period with respective stamp and seal
