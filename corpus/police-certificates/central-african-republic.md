# How to get a police certificate – Central African Republic
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your biometrics (fingerprints and photo) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Extrait du Casier Judiciaire
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your application package or instructions for details.
## How to apply
You can get a police certificate from the chief clerk of the country court at the Tribunal de Grande Instance in Bangui.
You’ll also need to 
  * provide a legal birth certificate
  * pay fees depending on your nationality (20,000 F)

## Contact information
Tribunal de Grande Instance de Bangui  
Boulevard Général de Gaulle, BP 884 Bangui
