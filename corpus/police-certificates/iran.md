# How to get a police certificate – Iran
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Certificate of no criminal record
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
**If you live in Iran**
You can apply
  * in person at a police+10 center
  * online via the [Sana System](https://judperson.adliran.ir/)
    * This page won’t work outside of Iran.

**If you live outside of Iran**
Apply at your nearest Iranian embassy.
**If you’re an Iranian living in Canada**
Due to the closure of the Iranian embassy in Canada, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Iran has provided an [alternative to applying for a certificate of no criminal record](https://mfa.gov.ir/portal/helpdeskdata/5033/347/%D8%B5%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1-%DA%AF%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%87%DB%8C-%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%85-%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%BE%DB%8C%D8%B4%DB%8C%D9%86%D9%87) (available in Persian only).
## Contact information
For up-to-date instructions on documents required, please consult the [Iranian police website](http://epolice.ir/) (in Persian only).
