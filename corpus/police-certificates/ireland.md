# How to get a police certificate – Ireland
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Police Certificate
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
Apply in person or by mail to the superintendent who is in charge of the district where you live or lived. 
  * There is no centralized police computer system serving county superintendent offices, and each county’s Garda Síochána office issues their own certificates.
  * Apply for a police certificate for each county have lived in during the time period stated in your application package or document checklist. 

For more information, visit the [Garda Síochána website](https://www.garda.ie/en/About-Us/Online-Services/Data-Protection-FOI-Police-Certificates/Police-Certificates.html).
## Contact information
Contact information for each district is available on the [Irish National Police Station Directory](https://www.garda.ie/en/Contact-Us/Station-Directory/).
