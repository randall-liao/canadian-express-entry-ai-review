# How to get a police certificate - Jordan
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
**Do I need to provide a police certificate when applying for permanent residence?**  
Yes.
**Name of document(s) to get** : Jordan has two different types of police certificates, the **Good Conduct certificate** and the **Non-conviction certificate**. **_Both certificates_** are required.
## How to get a Police Certificate:
### If you are a citizen of Jordan:
#### If you live in Jordan:
The **Good Conduct certificate** can be obtained at the General Intelligence Department. There are offices located in cities across Jordan.
The **Non-conviction certificate** can be obtained at the Courts of First Instance. There are offices located in cities across Jordan.
#### If you live outside of Jordan:
You can apply for the **Good Conduct certificate** and the **Non-conviction certificate** through any Jordanian embassy abroad. See [Information and documents you need](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/jordan.html#info) for a list of required documents.
### If you are not a citizen of Jordan:
#### If you live in Jordan:
The Good Conduct certificate can be obtained at the General Intelligence Department. There are offices located in cities across Jordan.
The Non-conviction certificate can be obtained at the Courts of First Instance. There are offices located in cities across Jordan.
#### If you live outside of Jordan:
You can apply for the Good Conduct certificate and the Non-conviction certificate through any Jordanian embassy abroad. See [Information and documents you need](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/jordan.html#info) for a list of required documents.
## Information and documents you need:
To get a **Good Conduct certificate** , you will need to provide:
  * A police certificate form for travel abroad,
  * two passport photographs (6cm x 4cm),
  * a copy of your passport, and
  * fee stamps

To get a **Non-conviction certificate** , you will need to provide:
  * A non-conviction form,
  * a valid identification document or passport,
  * application fees, and
  * fee stamps
