# How to Get a Police Certificate - Kyrgyzstan
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Reference certificate on presence or absence of criminal records (_Spravka o nalichii libo otsutstvii sudimosti_)
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you live in Kyrgyzstan
You should apply:
  * In person at your local Public Service Centre. You may also give a representative Power of Attorney to apply on your behalf
  * Through payment terminals connected to the system of the government electronic services.
  * On-line at [www.portal.tunduk.kg](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/www.portal.tunduk.kg)

### If you live outside of Kyrgyzstan
You can apply at the Consular Section of your nearest Embassy of Kyrgyzstan.
## Special considerations
The police certificate must indicate all names you have used while in Kyrgyzstan.
The Embassy only accepts original police certificates. Print-outs of online search results and electronic certificates are not acceptable.
