# How to Get a Police Certificate – Latvia
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Certificate of (non)-Criminal record
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you live in Latvia
Apply by email or post at the [Information Centre of the Ministry of Interior](https://www.ic.iem.gov.lv/en) (see [Contact Information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/latvia.html#spec) for complete address). Translation into English is also available at an additional cost.
### If you live outside of Latvia
Apply at your nearest Latvian embassy or consulate.
