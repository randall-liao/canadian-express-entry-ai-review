# How to Get a Police Certificate – Liechtenstein
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
**Do I need to provide a Police Certificate with my Application for Permanent Residence?** Yes
**Name of document(s) to get:** Extract from Central Police Records (_Auszug aus dem Strafregister_ or _Strafregisterbescheinigung)_
## How to get a Police Certificate: 
### If you are a citizen of Liechtenstein:
#### If you live in Liechtenstein: 
You can apply in person at the Landgericht (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/liechtenstein.html#spec) for complete address) or online through the [Landesverwaltung Furstentum website](http://www.llv.li/) (available in German only). 
#### If you live outside of Liechtenstein: 
Please follow the [procedures for Switzerland](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/switzerland.html). 
### If you are not a citizen of Liechtenstein:
#### If you live in Liechtenstein: 
You can apply in person at the Landgericht (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/liechtenstein.html#spec) for complete address) or online through the [Landesverwaltung Furstentum website](http://www.llv.li/) (available in German only).
#### If you live outside of Liechtenstein: 
Please follow the [procedures for Switzerland](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/switzerland.html). 
### Information and documents you need:
  * Official identification
  * Applicable fees (to be paid in CHF only)
