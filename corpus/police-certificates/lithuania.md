# How to Get a Police Certificate – Lithuania
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
_Teistumo (neteistumo) pažyma_
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you live in Lithuania
Apply to the Information Technology and Communications Department (see Contact Information for complete address).
### If you live outside of Lithuania
Apply at your nearest Lithuanian embassy or consulate.
### Documents you need
  * [Application form (PDF, 178.88 KB)](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/ircc/migration/ircc/english/information/security/police-cert/documents/pdf/prasymo_formos_pavyzdys_fiziniam_asmeniui.pdf) (in Lithuanian only)
  * Identification document (e.g. Passport or ID card) 
  * Applicable fees
