# How to get a police certificate - Mali
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of document you need
Police certificate
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence
  * citizenship
  * some temporary residence programs

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
**If you’re a Mali national**
You can get a police certificate in person at the Tribunal or the Prefecture (for locations without a tribunal) at the place of your birth. Please ensure that you bring your birth certificate and Mali identity card when you request your police certificate.
**If you’re a non-national or resident of Mali**
You can get a police certificate in person from the [Court of First Instance](https://mali.eregulations.org/Contacts/13?l=en) (check [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/mali.html#contact) for address).
