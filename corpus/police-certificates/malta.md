# How to get a police certificate – Malta
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Certifikat tal-Kondotta/Certificate of Conduct (Form A)
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
[Apply online](https://kondotti.gov.mt/) with the Malta Police Force or in person at the Criminal Records Office in Floriana (check [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/malta.html#contact) for complete details).
If you’re a citizen of Malta, you must sign in to the Government of Malta’s [identification portal](https://eid.gov.mt/auth/Account/Login) with your e-ID account and Maltese ID card or residence permit. For information on how to get an e-ID account, please check the [frequently asked questions](https://eid.gov.mt/auth/Help#eid-register-anchor).
