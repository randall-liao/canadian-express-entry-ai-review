# How to Get a Police Certificate - Moldova
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
**Do I need to provide a Police Certificate with my Application for Permanent Residence?** Yes
**Name of document(s) to get:** Police Certificate (_Certificat de Cazier judiciar_); it is issued in Romanian or Russian
## How to get a Police Certificate
### If you are a citizen of Moldova
#### If you live in Moldova: 
You can apply:
  * [online](http://servicii.gov.md/ecazier) (in Romanian only), 
  * at branches of [Banca de Economii](http://bem.md/top/network) and [Victoriabank](http://www.victoriabank.md/ro/filiala/);
  * at payment terminals [QIWI](http://qiwi.md/ru/private/address/) (in Romanian only);
  * at your local District Police Station;
  * at the central issuing institution, [Serviciul tehnologii informaţionale al MAI](http://www.mai.gov.md/content/565) (in Romanian only).

The police certificates can be collected only in person or by a representative (with a notarized Power of Attorney letter):
  * from [Serviciul tehnologii informaţionale al MAI](http://www.mai.gov.md/content/565) (in Romanian only); 
  * at your local District Police Station.

#### If you live outside of Moldova: 
You should submit your application to the consular section of your nearest Republic of Moldova Embassy. The application and fees are to be accompanied by a copy of your passport page containing your photograph and identifying information.
Alternatively, you can authorize, through a notarized Power of Attorney letter, a person residing in Moldova to submit an application on your behalf. 
You can also [apply online](http://servicii.gov.md/ecazier) (in Romanian only) and authorize, through a notarized Power of Attorney letter, a person residing in Moldova to collect your police certificate.
### If you are not a citizen of Moldova
#### If you live in Moldova:
You can apply at the central issuing institution, [Serviciul tehnologii informaţionale al MAI](http://www.mai.gov.md/content/565) (in Romanian only) - (see _[Special notes and considerations](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/moldova.html#notes)_ for complete address).
The police certificates can be collected only in person or by a representative (with a notarized Power of Attorney letter) from [Serviciul tehnologii informaţionale al MAI](http://www.mai.gov.md/content/565) (in Romanian only).
#### If you live outside of Moldova:
You should submit your application to the consular section of the nearest Republic of Moldova Embassy. The application and fees are to be accompanied by a copy of your passport page containing your photograph and identifying information.
Alternatively, you can authorize, through a notarized Power of Attorney letter, a person residing in Moldova to submit an application on your behalf. 
### Information and documents you need
  * Moldovan identity card 
  * Passport (for citizens of other countries)
  * Proof of processing fee payment
  * Completed Application for a Police Clearance Certificate (submitted online on the portal of [servicii.gov.md](http://servicii.gov.md/), at Banca de Economii or Victoriabank, at payment terminals or directly at Serviciul tehnologii informaţionale al MAI)
  * Notarized power of attorney (if applying through a third party).
