# How to get a police certificate – New Caledonia
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Bulletin no. 3
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for permanent residence in Canada.
## How to apply
Consult the profile for [France](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/france.html).
## Contact information
Further information can be found on the [Ministère de la Justice](https://casier-judiciaire.justice.gouv.fr/mai-web-b3-presentation/pages/accueil.xhtml?cid=1) website.
