# How to get a police certificate - Niger
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Bulletin no 3 du casier judiciaire
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you live in Niger
#### If you’re a citizen of Niger
You must send an application for a police clearance certificate (Extrait du casier judiciaire) by mail to the court of appeal ([Cour d’appel](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/niger.html#contact)).
#### If you’re a foreign or former resident
You must send an application to the senior registrar of the [court of appeal (greffier en chef de la Cour d’appel)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/niger.html#contact). You can find more information and apply online on Niger’s [administrative procedures website](https://www.service-public.ne/en/node/433).
### If you live outside Niger
Your application must include a copy of your identity document certified by an embassy or consulate of Niger. You can either
  * submit the application yourself
  * designate someone you know or someone with notarized authorization to submit it for you

Mail your application to the Cour d’appel (check [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/niger.html#contact)).
