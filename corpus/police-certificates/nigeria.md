# How to get a police certificate – Nigeria
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Police Character Certificate
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police character certificate when you apply for permanent residence in Canada.
## How to apply
There are 2 ways that you can apply:
  1. You can apply in person to the Central Criminal Registry.

You must bring
  * your valid passport
  * 2 passport-size photographs
  * the applicable fees

  1. You can apply by mail to the commissioner of police.

You must send
  * a prepaid return courier envelope
  * a copy of your passport photo page
  * a set of fingerprints taken and certified by a recognized police authority or the Nigerian embassy or consulate
  * an international money order payable to the Nigerian Police Force, C.I.D. (Criminal Investigation Department)

**Note:** Police certificates can be certified at your nearest Nigerian embassy or consulate for a fee.
