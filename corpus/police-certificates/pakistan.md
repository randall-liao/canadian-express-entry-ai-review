# How to Get a Police Certificate - Pakistan
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Police Character Certificate or Police Clearance Certificate
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you are a citizen of Pakistan
#### If you live in Pakistan
There are similar, but different, procedures for different provinces of Pakistan.
  * [Islambad](https://Islamabadpolice.gov.pk/character-certificate.php)
  * [Sindh](https://prvs.sindhpolice.gov.pk/site/addRequest)
  * [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://kppolice.gov.pk/detail.php?pid=52)
    * These agencies and tribal areas of the Federally Administered Tribal Area (FATA) have been merged in the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province:
      * Khyber
      * Mohmand
      * Bajaur
      * Orakzai
      * Kurram
      * North Waziristan
      * South Waziristan
  * [Balochistan](https://pkm.balochistanpolice.gov.pk)
  * [Punjab](https://pkm.punjab.gov.pk/public/app/our_services?id=a)
    * The Punjab province has introduced separate facilitation offices, Police Khidmat Markaz (PKM), for the public.
      * This service is available in all 36 districts of the province, including multiple offices in major metropolitan cities like Lahore, Faisalabad and Rawalpindi.
    * You can now apply for a Character Certificate from any center all over Punjab. You don’t need to apply from the district for which a character certificate is required.
      * For example, if you’re currently living in Lahore and previously lived in Rawalpindi, you can apply for a Character Certificate directly from the PKM in Lahore.

In-person processing is only done in [**Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province**](https://www.kppolice.gov.pk/detail.php?pid=52). You may need to visit the Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) office.
After processing, a certificate:
  * will be delivered to your mailing address via courier, or
  * can be collected from the same office you applied at.

#### If you live outside of Pakistan:
Contact your nearest Embassy or Consulate of Pakistan for assistance. You should bring:
  * A completed [application form (PDF, 0.6 MB)](https://www.pakconsulate.ca/assets/downloads/pcc-4.pdf)
  * Certified copies of your passport, including all the pages showing your legal stay
  * Certified copy of your National Identity Card
  * 3 passport-size photographs
  * Applicable fees

In some cases, a relative who lives in Pakistan can apply on your behalf. They need **all of the above items** and they need:
  * To fill in the [application form (PDF, 0.6 MB)](https://www.pakconsulate.ca/assets/downloads/pcc-4.pdf)
  * To write an affidavit (on 20Rs judicial stamp paper) stating that they will receive your certificate on your behalf
    * [Example of an affidavit (PDF, 0.8 MB)](https://islamabadpolice.gov.pk/img/Affidavit.pdf)
  * An authority letter, in their name, to get your certificate on your behalf
    * [Example of an authority letter (PDF, 0.1 MB)](https://www.pakconsulate.ca/assets/downloads/authority_letter.pdf)

### If you are not a citizen of Pakistan:
#### If you live in Pakistan:
Apply directly at the local district office of the Senior Superintendent of Police of the Police Security Branch. You may need to present:
  * A completed [application form (PDF, 0.6 MB)](https://www.pakconsulate.ca/assets/downloads/pcc-4.pdf)
  * Certified photocopies of your passport, including all the pages showing your legal stay
  * Certified photocopy of your national identity card (if applicable)
  * 3 passport-size photographs
  * Applicable fees

#### If you live outside of Pakistan:
Apply at your nearest Embassy or Consulate for Pakistan. You may need:
  * A completed [application form (PDF, 0.6 MB)](https://www.pakconsulate.ca/assets/downloads/pcc-4.pdf)
  * Certified photocopies of your passport, including all the pages showing your legal stay in Pakistan
  * Certified photocopy of your national identity card (if applicable)
  * 3 passport-size photographs
  * Applicable fees

### Special considerations
**Note** : The Canadian High Commission in Islamabad **cannot** help you get a police certificate.
