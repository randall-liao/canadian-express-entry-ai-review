# How to Get a Police Certificate - Paraguay
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Certificado de Antecedentes
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you are a citizen of Paraguay
#### If you live in Paraguay
Apply [online](https://www.paraguay.gov.py/) (in Spanish only) or at the Policía Nacional, Departamento de Identificaciones in Asunción in person or through a representative authorized in writing to act on your behalf.
#### If you live outside of Paraguay
Apply through the Paraguayan embassy or consulate, or you can fax or mail a copy of your cedula and a written authorization to a close relative (your father, mother, son, daughter or spouse) to apply on your behalf. Instructions and more information is available on the [Government of Paraguay](https://www.paraguay.gov.py/) website (in Spanish only).
### If you are not a citizen of Paraguay
#### If you live in Paraguay
Apply at the Policía Nacional, Departamento de Identificaciones in Asunción in person or through a representative authorized in writing to act on your behalf. Instructions and more information is available on the [Government of Paraguay](https://www.paraguay.gov.py/) website (in Spanish only).
#### If you live outside of Paraguay
Apply through the Paraguayan embassy or consulate.
### Special considerations
Only certificates issued by the National Police headquarters (Policia Nacional) are accepted. Certificates issued by any other federal/provincial government offices are not accepted.
