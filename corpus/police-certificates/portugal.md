# How to get a police certificate – Portugal
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of document you need
« Certificado do Registo Criminal » (Criminal Record Certificate)
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you live in Portugal
#### Apply online
Use the [Online Criminal Record](https://registocriminal.justica.gov.pt/?&request_locale=en) service to apply online. You’ll need your citizen card with the Digital Mobile Key (Chave Móvel Digital or CMD) that you got with your citizen card.
#### Apply in person
Go to any of these locations to apply in person:
  * Central authority desks: [Balcões dos Serviços de Identificação Criminal](http://www.dgaj.mj.pt/sections/files/identificacao-criminal/identificacao-criminal/sections/files/identificacao-criminal/identificacao-criminal/balcoes-dos-servicos-de/downloadFile/file/Balcoes-Servicos-Identificacao-Criminal.pdf?nocache=1469725518.95) (in Portuguese only) (PDF)
  * Courts: [Unidades Centrais ou Juízos de Proximidade de Secretarias de Tribunais de Comarca](http://www.dgaj.mj.pt/sections/files/identificacao-criminal/identificacao-criminal/sections/files/identificacao-criminal/identificacao-criminal/unidades-centrais-ou/downloadFile/file/Unidades_centrais_seccoes_proximidade_tribunais.pdf?nocache=1491559529.56) (in Portuguese only) (PDF)
  * Citizen service offices that offer [Espaços do Cidadão](https://www2.gov.pt/en/locais-de-atendimento-de-servicos-publicos)
  * Residents of the Azores can apply to the local citizen services office: [Lojas da Rede Integrada de Apoio ao Cidadão](https://www.riac.azores.gov.pt/#!/lojas) (in Portuguese only) (RIAC) da Região Autónoma dos Açores

You don’t need an application form, but you must prove your identity with acceptable photo identification such as a citizen card, identification card or other valid identification document.
### If you live outside of Portugal
#### Apply online
Use the [Online Criminal Record](https://registocriminal.justica.gov.pt/?&request_locale=en) service to apply online if you have an active citizen card code and reader, or with the Digital Mobile Key (Chave Móvel Digital or CMD) that you got with your citizen card.
You must have a bank account in Portugal with a home banking service.
#### Apply by email, mail or fax
Fill out the [Request for a Portuguese Criminal Record Certificate](http://www.dgaj.mj.pt/sections/files/identificacao-criminal/download-de-impressos/sections/files/identificacao-criminal/download-de-impressos/formulario/downloadFile/file/2-Formulario_ReqCertRegistCriminal_jul2016.pdf?nocache=1469702200.66) (PDF) form and send it to the [Criminal Identification Services of the DGAJ](http://www.dgaj.mj.pt/sections/files/infosite/criminal-and/). See [Special notes and considerations](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/portugal.html#notes) for the complete address.
## Information and documents you need
  * Valid identity card, residence card or passport
  * Reason for the request (e.g. Immigration purposes)
  * Applicable fees
