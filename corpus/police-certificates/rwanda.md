# How to Get a Police Certificate - Rwanda
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
**Do I need to provide a Police Certificate with my Application for Permanent Residence?** Yes
**Name of document(s) to get:** Extrait du Casier Judiciaire
## How to get a Police Certificate
### If you are a citizen of Rwanda
#### If you live in Rwanda: 
You should apply in person to the National Public Prosecution Authority – Department of Criminal Record Clearance Certificate. You must bring:
  * A copy of your identity card or its replacement for those who have lost it
  * Two recent passport size photographs
  * A copy of an employment letter, an employment contract or an employment card (for those wanting it for employment reasons)
  * Applicable fees

#### If you live outside of Rwanda: 
You should apply at your nearest Embassy of Rwanda. You must bring:
  * A copy of a valid Rwandan passport
  * A copy of a valid visa (for those living in countries within the East African Community (EAC) and Communauté économique de Pays de Grands Lacs (CEPGL)
  * A residence permit
  * Two recent passport size photographs
  * Applicable fees

### If you are not a citizen of Rwanda
#### If you live in Rwanda: 
You should apply in person to the National Public Prosecution Authority – Department of Criminal Record Clearance Certificate. You must bring:
  * A copy of your valid passport 
  * A copy of a Rwandan visa 
  * Two recent passport size photographs 
  * A copy of an employment letter, an employment contract or an employment card (for those wanting it for employment reasons)
  * Applicable fees

#### If you live outside of Rwanda:
You should apply at your nearest Embassy of Rwanda. You must bring:
  * A copy of a valid passport
  * A copy of a valid visa (if you are a foreigner living in a country in which you are not a citizen)
  * A document showing that you once lived in Rwanda
  * Two recent passport size photographs
  * Applicable fees

### Information and documents you need:
  * All information and documents needed are explained above

### Special notes and considerations
National Public Prosecution Authority – Department of Criminal Code  
P.O. Box 1328  
Kigali, Rwanda
  * Applying and collecting the certificate is done by the concerned applicant
  * No fingerprints required for all applicants whether you live in Rwanda or outside Rwanda
  * Additional information is available at the [National Public Prosecution Authority](http://nppa.gov.rw/home/?L=1) website. Website is in English and Kinyarwanda.
