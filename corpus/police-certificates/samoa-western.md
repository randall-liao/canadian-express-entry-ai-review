# How to Get a Police Certificate - Samoa (Western)
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
**Do I need to provide a Police Certificate with my Application for Permanent Residence?** Yes
**Name of document(s) to get:** Police Certificate
## How to get a Police Certificate
### If you are a citizen of Samoa (Western)
#### If you live in Samoa (Western): 
You should apply by mail to the Police, Prisons and Fire Services (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/samoa-western.html#notes) for complete address).
#### If you live outside of Samoa (Western): 
You should apply by mail to Police, Prisons and Fire Services (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/samoa-western.html#notes) for complete address).
### If you are not a citizen of Samoa (Western)
#### If you live in Samoa (Western):
You should apply by mail to Police, Prisons and Fire Services (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/samoa-western.html#notes) for complete address).
#### If you live outside of Samoa (Western):
You should apply by mail to Police, Prisons and Fire Services (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/samoa-western.html#notes) for complete address).
### Information and documents you need
  * Certified copy of birth certificate 
  * Passport-size photograph
  * Applicable fees
  * Full set of fingerprints if applying from outside Samoa

### Special notes and considerations
