# How to get a police certificate – Seychelles
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
**Do I need to provide a Police Certificate with my Application for Permanent Residence?** Yes
**Name of document(s) to get:** Character Certificate
## How to get a Police Certificate:
### If you are a citizen of Seychelles:
#### If you live in Seychelles:
You should apply in person to the Commissioner of Police (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/seychelles.html#spec) for complete address). You must provide:
  * Identification documents: birth certificate or passport
  * Must allow fingerprints to be taken
  * Applicable fees

#### If you live outside of Seychelles:
You should apply in writing to the Commissioner of Police (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/seychelles.html#spec) for complete address). You must provide:
  * Copies of your birth certificate and passport
  * The address where you lived while in Seychelles 
  * Date you left Seychelles
  * Applicable fees

### If you are not a citizen of Seychelles:
#### If you live in Seychelles:
You should apply in person to the Commissioner of Police (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/seychelles.html#spec) for complete address). You must provide: 
  * Copies of your passport and proof of legal stay in Seychelles, visa/permit
  * Copy of your birth certificate
  * Must allow fingerprints to be taken
  * Applicable fees

#### If you live outside of Seychelles:
You should apply in writing to the Commissioner of Police (see [_Special notes and considerations_](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/seychelles.html#spec) for complete address). You must provide:
  * Copies of your birth certificate and passport
  * The address where you lived in Seychelles
  * Date you left Seychelles
  * Applicable fees

### Information and documents you need: 
  * All information and documentation needed are explained above.
