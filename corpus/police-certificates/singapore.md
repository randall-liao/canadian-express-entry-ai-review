# How to get a police certificate – Singapore
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Certificate of Clearance (COC)
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
We will send you a letter requesting a police certificate **after** you send us your application if a COC is required.
You must **include that letter** and submit your application online to the Criminal Investigation Department of the [Singapore Police Force](https://eservices1.police.gov.sg/phub/eservices/landingpage/apply-pay-for-coc).
The Singapore Police Force will send your police clearance certificate directly to the IRCC office at the Consulate General of Canada.
## Contact information
All applications and payments for a COC are to be made online. The COC office does not accept walk-in applications.
