# How to get a police certificate – South Africa
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Police Clearance Certificate
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you live in South Africa
Apply in person at your local South African Police Service office. Check the [South African Police Service](https://www.saps.gov.za/services/applying_clearence_certificate.php) website for full instructions.
### If you live outside of South Africa
You can apply at your nearest South African embassy or by mail to the [South African Police Service office](https://www.saps.gov.za/services/applying_clearence_certificate.php) (check [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/south-africa.html#contact) for the full address).
## Special considerations
Certificates from Ideco Biometrics are **not** valid for immigration purposes. Applicants should always request the standard Republic of South Africa police clearance certificate.
