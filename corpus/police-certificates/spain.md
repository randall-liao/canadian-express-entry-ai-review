# How to get a Police Certificate – Spain (including the Canary Islands)
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Criminal Record Certificate (Certificado de Antecedentes Penales)
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence
  * citizenship
  * some temporary residence programs

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
You must complete a [Model 790 application (PDF, 909 KB)](https://sede.mjusticia.gob.es/servidorformularios/formularios?idFormulario=790&lang=es_es&idtramite=1288774398320&idpagina=1215197884559) and follow the instructions on the [Ministry of Justice website](https://www.mjusticia.gob.es/es/ciudadania/tramites/certificado-antecedentes).
## Special considerations
### Do I need a police certificate for International Experience Canada?
If you’re a citizen of Spain, you don’t need to provide a police certificate from this country with your application. However, an IRCC officer may request one at a later date.
You **must** provide police certificates for **all other** countries or territories where you have lived for a total of six months or more since your 18th birthday.
