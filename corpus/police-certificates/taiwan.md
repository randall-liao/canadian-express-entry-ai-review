# How to get a police certificate – Taiwan
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Police Criminal Record Certificate
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
You need to apply through the Taipei City Police Department if you live
  * in Taipei
  * outside Taiwan

You can apply
  * online
  * in person
  * by mail
  * fax

Check the [Taipei City Police Department website](https://english.police.gov.taipei/cp.aspx?n=E3E7C79E91794CF8) for downloadable application forms and detailed instructions, including required documentation and applicable fees.
If you live in another part of Taiwan, you need to apply at your local City Police Department.
