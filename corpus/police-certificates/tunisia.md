# How to Get a Police Certificate - Tunisia
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
**Do I need to provide a Police Certificate with my Application for Permanent Residence?** Yes
**Name of document(s) to get** : [Bulletin no. 3](https://b3.interieur.gov.tn/) (available in French and Arabic only)
## How to get a Police Certificate:
### If you are a citizen of Tunisia:
#### If you live in Tunisia:
You can apply at your local police station or online at the [Tunisian Republic Ministry of the Interior website](https://b3.interieur.gov.tn/) (available in French and Arabic only).
#### If you live outside of Tunisia:
You can apply at your local police station or online at the [Tunisian Republic Ministry of the Interior website](https://b3.interieur.gov.tn/) (available in French and Arabic only).
### If you are not a citizen of Tunisia:
#### If you live in Tunisia: 
You can apply at your local police station or online at the [Tunisian Republic Ministry of the Interior website](https://b3.interieur.gov.tn/) (available in French and Arabic only).
#### If you live outside of Tunisia:
You may apply to the Bureau d’accueil et d’orientation du consulat at the [Tunisian Republic Ministry of the Interior website](https://b3.interieur.gov.tn/) (available in French and Arabic only).
#### Information and documents you need:
  * National Identity card or document (original and a copy)
  * Birth certificate
  * Applicable fees

### Special notes and considerations
Request forms are available [in Arabic](http://www.atunisie-ch.org/data/dataimages/Upload/B3.pdf) and [in French](http://www.atunisie-ch.org/data/dataimages/Upload/B3-Fr.pdf)
