# How to get a police certificate - Turkmenistan
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Police Certificate
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you are a citizen of Turkmenistan
#### If you live in Turkmenistan
Apply at either the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Ministry of Interior (see [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/turkmenistan.html#SpecialNotes) for complete addresses).
#### If you live outside of Turkmenistan
Apply to the Consular Section of your nearest Embassy of Turkmenistan.
### If you are not a citizen of Turkmenistan
#### If you live in Turkmenistan
Apply at either the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Ministry of Interior (see [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/turkmenistan.html#SpecialNotes) for complete addresses).
#### If you live outside of Turkmenistan
Apply to the Consular Section of your nearest Embassy of Turkmenistan. You will need to provide details of where you lived before Turkmenistan.
### Information and documents you need
  * Valid passport
  * Applicable fees
