# How to get a police certificate – Uzbekistan
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Certificate
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence
  * citizenship
  * some temporary residence programs

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you live in Uzbekistan:
You should apply online at <https://my.gov.uz/ru> (available in Russian and Uzbek) or in person at a local service centre. When the certificate is ready, you should collect it in person at one of the service centres. You may also give a representative power of attorney to collect it on your behalf.
### If you live outside of Uzbekistan:
You can apply at the Consular Section of your nearest Embassy of Uzbekistan.
## Special considerations
The police certificate must indicate all names you have used while in Uzbekistan.
The Embassy accepts only original police certificates. Print-outs of online search results and electronic certificates are not acceptable.
