# How to Get a Police Certificate - Vanuatu
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Police Clearance Certificate
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence
  * citizenship
  * some temporary residence programs

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
You should apply in person or [online](https://police.gov.vu/what-we-do/other-units/criminal-records/110-police-clearance-steps-to-follow) at the Commissioner of Police (see [Contact Information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/vanuatu.html#contact) for complete address).
## Special considerations
Vanuatu Police Clearance certificate will be issued for non-citizens residing in Vanuatu and also non-citizens who entered Vanuatu as a short or long stay visitor.
A Vanuatu Police Clearance certificate will not be issued to a Vanuatu Citizen who has obtained Vanuatu citizenship through the Vanuatu Development Program, but never entered or resided in Vanuatu.
