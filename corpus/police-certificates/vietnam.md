# How to get a police certificate – Vietnam
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Judicial Certificate #2 or Criminal Record Certificate #2 (Phieu Ly Lich Tu Phap So 2)
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you are a Vietnamese national
You can either:
  * apply in person or by mail to the Department of Justice office located in the district where your permanent address is registered
  * apply online through the Public Service Portal for your area
    * The Public Service Portal website will take the following format: [https://dichvucong.PROVINCENAME.gov.vn](https://dichvucong.provincename.gov.vn/) (example: [http://dichvucong.angiang.gov.vn](http://dichvucong.angiang.gov.vn/)).

#### If you live in Hanoi
If you apply online, you must submit your application via the [Online Judicial Certification Registration Service of Department of Justice of Hanoi City](https://dichvucong.hanoi.gov.vn/) (Dich vu dang ky cap phieu ly lich tu phap truc Tuyen – So Tu Phap Thanh pho Ha Noi). 
If your permanent residence records have been annulled pursuant [Article 29, the Residence Law 2020](https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=146648&Keyword=lu%E1%BA%ADt%20c%C6%B0%20tr%C3%BA%202020) (available in Vietnamese only), you must:
  * get a confirmation letter from the district police in your last place of permanent residence which states that your permanent residence registration was deleted

### If you are not a Vietnamese national
#### _If you live in Hanoi, Vietnam_
Apply through the [Online Judicial Certification Registration Service of Department of Justice of Hanoi City](https://dichvucong.hanoi.gov.vn/) (Dich vu dang ky cap phieu ly lich tu phap truc Tuyen – So Tu Phap Thanh pho Ha Noi).
#### _If you live in a city other than Hanoi, Vietnam_
You can either:
  * apply in person or by mail to the Department of Justice office located in the district where your permanent address is registered
  * apply online through the Public Service Portal for your area
    * The Public Service Portal website will take the following format: [https://dichvucong.PROVINCENAME.gov.vn](https://dichvucong.provincename.gov.vn/) (example: [http://dichvucong.angiang.gov.vn](http://dichvucong.angiang.gov.vn/)).

#### _If you no longer live in Vietnam_
You can apply in person or by mail to the National Center for Judicial Records (Trung Tam Ly Lich Tu Phap Quoc Gia) (check [Contact Information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/vietnam.html#contact) for the complete address).
