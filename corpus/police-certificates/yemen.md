# How to get a police certificate – Yemen
If you need to give your fingerprints for a police certificate, this **isn’t** the same as giving your [biometrics (fingerprints and photo)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/campaigns/biometrics/facts.html) for an application.
## Name of the document you need
Criminal Status Sheet
## Police certificate and IRCC requirements
You must provide a police certificate when you apply for:
  * permanent residence
  * citizenship
  * some temporary residence programs

Check your [application package or instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides.html) for details.
## How to apply
### If you are a citizen of Yemen
#### If you live in Yemen
Apply in person at the Office of the Deputy General Director (see [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/yemen.html#spec) for complete address). You must bring:
  * A completed police clearance request form
  * Two photographs (4x6 cm) with a white background
  * A copy of your valid ID card/passport
  * Applicable fees

#### If you live outside of Yemen
You can:
  1. Return to Yemen and apply in person
  2. Ask a representative (a friend, relative or colleague) in Yemen, with a power of attorney, to apply on your behalf at the Office of the Deputy General Director (see [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/yemen.html#spec) for complete address)

You must bring:
  * A completed police clearance request form
  * Two photographs (4x6 cm) with a white background
  * A copy of your valid ID card/passport
  * Applicable fees

### If you are a not a citizen of Yemen
#### If you live in Yemen (for more than 6 months)
Apply in person at the Office of the Deputy General Director (see [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/yemen.html#spec) for complete address). You must bring:
  * The letter from the Embassy of Canada requesting the  _Criminal Status Sheet_
  * A completed police clearance request form
  * Two photographs (4x6 cm) with a white background
  * A copy of your valid passport
  * A copy of your valid residence visa for Yemen
  * Applicable fees

#### If you live outside of Yemen (but have lived in Yemen for more than 6 months)
You can:
  1. Return to Yemen and apply in person
  2. Ask a representative (a friend, relative or colleague) in Yemen, with a power of attorney, to apply on your behalf at the Office of the Deputy General Director (see [Contact information](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/medical-police/police-certificates/how/yemen.html#spec) for complete address)

You must bring:
  * The letter from the Embassy of Canada requesting the  _Criminal Status Sheet_
  * A completed police clearance request form
  * Two photographs (4x6 cm) with a white background
  * A copy of your valid passport
  * A copy of all former residence visas for Yemen
  * Applicable fees
