# Canadian Experience Class: Assessing against selection criteria
Officers will assess the applicant against each of the following eligibility requirements, based on the information and documents provided in the application [R87.1]:
  * [Temporary resident status](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/permanent-residence/economic-classes/experience/temporary-resident-status.html)
  * [Official language proficiency](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/permanent-residence/economic-classes/experience/official-language-proficiency.html)
  * [Qualifying skilled work experience in Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/permanent-residence/economic-classes/experience/qualifying-work-experience.html)
  * [Education requirement - for Post-Graduate stream only](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/permanent-residence/economic-classes/experience/education-requirement-post-graduation-stream.html).

**Note:** applicants must intend to reside in a province or territory other than Quebec to be eligible under this class.
