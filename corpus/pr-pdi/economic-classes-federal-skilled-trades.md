# Federal skilled trades class (FSTC)
This section describes how to process electronic applications for permanent residence submitted by federal skilled trades class (FSTC) applicants who were invited to apply through Express Entry.
The FSTC is prescribed as a class of people who are qualified skilled trades workers, who may become permanent residents based on their ability to become economically established in Canada and who intend to reside in a province other than Quebec.
