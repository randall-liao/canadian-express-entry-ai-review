# Federal skilled workers selection criteria: Education
## Awarding points: Education
For an applicant to be assigned points under subsection [R78(1)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227/section-78.html), their educational credentials must
  * be Canadian educational credentials as in subsection [R73(1)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227/section-73.html)
  * or
  * have an educational credential equivalency assessment (ECA) of their foreign educational credentials issued by an organization or institution designated under subsection [R75(4)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227/section-75.html)

Officers should assess the application and award the applicant up to 25 points for education, as follows:  
Table: Educational credentials  
| Educational credential  | Points  |  
| --- | --- |  
| University-level credential at the doctoral level [R78(1)(_g_)]  | 25  |  
| University-level credential at the master’s level **or** an entry-to-practice professional degree. IRCC only accepts as an entry-to-practice professional degree those degrees issued in relation to an occupation listed in the NOC TEER Category 1 and for which licensing by a provincial regulatory body is required, in one of the following fields of study: 
  * chiropractic medicine
  * dentistry
  * law
  * medicine
  * optometry
  * pharmacy
  * podiatry
  * veterinary medicine

 | 23  |  
| 2 or more post-secondary program credentials, at least 1 of which must have been issued on completion of a post-secondary program of 3 years or longer [R78(1)(_e_)]  | 22  |  
| Post-secondary program credential of 3 years or longer [R78(1)(_d_)]  | 21  |  
| 2-year post-secondary program credential [R78(1)(_c_)]  | 19  |  
| 1-year post-secondary program credential [R78(1)(_b_)]  | 15  |  
| Secondary school (high school) credential [R78(1)(_a_)]  | 5  |  
**Note:** Paragraph R78(2)(_a_) provides that, except as set out in paragraph R78(1)(_e_) where points are awarded for 2 or more post-secondary educational credentials, points shall not be awarded cumulatively on the basis of more than 1 educational credential.
