# Medical requirements

You must complete an immigration medical examination before you come to Canada. This applies to all permanent residence applicants under Express Entry. A panel physician performs the exam and sends the results directly to IRCC. Do not send the results yourself. Your medical exam is valid for a limited period after which you may need to repeat it.

## How to book

Book your exam with a panel physician approved by IRCC. Bring valid identification and any prior medical records you have. The physician records the results electronically and transmits them securely.
